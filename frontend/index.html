The Retail Operational Challenge

A shipment delay is more than a logistics problem.

Retail supply chains handle thousands of concurrent product lines across distribution hubs and stores. When disruptions occur, operations teams face fundamental visibility and coordination hurdles.

Limited visibility

An inbound carrier delay alert does not automatically reveal every affected downstream warehouse, regional store, or customer order. Disconnected spreadsheets delay triage.

Uncertain impact

Available inventory, local demand rates, and transit ETAs change constantly. Teams struggle to calculate which regional depot will run empty first and how many stockout hours will occur.

Slow recovery decisions

Comparing inter-warehouse transfers, expedited air freight, and secondary suppliers under real-world lead times, budget constraints, and safety policies takes hours of manual coordination.

Standardized Disruption Workflow

From detection to auditable recovery.

A structured five-step lifecycle ensuring every recommendation is anchored in verifiable evidence and deterministic validation.

1

Detect or receive

Continuously monitor telemetry, carrier EDI feeds, and rule violations — or ingest direct user-reported operational alerts.

2

Investigate

Cross-reference EDI 214 signals, WMS bin counts, supplier statements, and vessel telemetry while flagging conflicting or stale data.

3

Assess impact

Calculate differential runway days across receiving facilities and model projected stockout hours and financial exposure.

4

Plan recovery

Synthesize and compare recovery options by lead time, transportation cost, shortage reduction percentage, and policy constraints.

5

Review & track

Present recommendations with trade-offs for human operational approval, record audit trails, and track execution milestones.

Specialized Multi-Agent Roles

Modular agents with clear operational duties.

Rather than a generic conversational bot, SupplyGuard deploys specialized functional components working in concert.

Coordinator

Case Lifecycle & Orchestration

Manages the disruption incident workspace, delegates sub-tasks, coordinates between specialist components, and maintains case status.

Disruption Investigator

Fact Extraction & Telemetry

Establishes what happened, retrieves carrier EDI updates, reconciles contradictory tracking data, and catalogs supporting evidence items.

Impact Assessor

Runway & Shortage Modeling

Models stock depletion across distribution networks, calculates days of coverage, and forecasts shortage volumes and financial exposure.

Recovery Planner

Strategy Formulation

Formulates and compares alternatives — inter-warehouse balancing, expedited freight, secondary suppliers, or passive monitoring.

Decision Reviewer

Constraint & Policy Validation

Validates proposed actions against corporate safety-stock mandates, flags budgetary trade-offs, and prepares the final sign-off brief.

Deterministic Service Guarantees: Core mathematical operations (runway calculation, safety-stock rule checks, cost calculations) are executed by deterministic, testable business logic engines rather than probabilistic text generation.
